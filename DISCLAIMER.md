# Disclaimer

**This software is for entertainment and hobby use only. It is not a weather
alerting system and must not be relied on in a weather emergency.**

The program shows a short version of this the first time it runs and won't
start until you tick a box saying you've read and understood it.

## Not for alerting the public

This program is not designed, intended or tested to alert the public about
dangerous weather. Do not use it as a public warning system (for example on a
cable channel, broadcast, website, school, business or emergency management
display), and do not use it as anyone's main source of weather warnings.

## Why: there is no redundancy

- It depends on one internet connection, one computer, the National Weather
  Service's servers and other free third-party services. Any of them can be
  slow, wrong or down, often exactly when severe weather hits.
- When that happens, a warning such as a Tornado Warning may show up late,
  incomplete, or not at all, and the display may give no sign that anything
  was missed.
- Nobody is watching it. The National Weather Service, broadcast
  meteorologists and services like NOAA Weather Radio have people working
  around the clock and several independent ways of getting warnings to the
  public. This program has neither.

## Where to get warnings

Use official sources for weather alerts and warnings:

- NOAA Weather Radio
- Wireless Emergency Alerts on your phone
- Local TV and radio
- [weather.gov](https://www.weather.gov) and your local emergency management agency
